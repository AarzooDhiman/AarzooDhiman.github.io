On-device AI for breast cancer MDTs

University of Hull with Hull University Teaching Hospitals NHS Foundation Trust · 2025 to 2026 · Co-Investigator, lead and corresponding author

  • Built and evaluated a speech recognition, LLM and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) pipeline on one NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin, so meeting audio stays on the device.
  • Cut Whisper word error rate by 20.7% and 24.4% on held-out recordings of real MDT meetings.
  • Local MedGemma-RAG recalled 0.318 of MDT-concordant treatments against 0.136 for a cloud chatbot baseline (p = 0.020).
  • Funded by a £41,707 NHS-funded Humber and North Yorkshire award.

COVID-19 inside UK homes

University College London · 2021 to 2023 · First author

  • Fine-tuned three CT-BERT classifiers with Hugging Face Transformers on 7,894 labelled tweets (10-fold cross-validation; AUC 0.907, 0.934, 0.768).
  • Built the pipeline from 1.23M candidate users to a geolocated UK cohort of 58,555 users and 107.9M tweets.
  • Household secondary attack rate 0.16 (95% CI 0.14–0.18), mean serial interval 6.49 days, and about 44% of household infections never posted about.

Nothing leaves the room

A breast cancer team meeting, heard, searched and summarised on one small computer in the room. Scroll through the four steps.

Swipe through the four steps.

  1. Listen

    Whisper large-v3 transcribes the meeting on the device. Silence-aware preprocessing, Bayesian search with Optuna and noise-adaptive decoding cut the word error rate by 20.7% and 24.4% on held-out recordings of real MDT meetings.

  2. Retrieve

    Each case is matched against NICE guidelines held in a local FAISS index, so a suggestion can point to the guidance behind it.

  3. Reason

    A quantised MedGemma 27B, served with llama.cpp on CUDA and TensorRT inside Docker, drafts the options. Audio and transcripts stay on the device, in step with NHS information governance.

  4. Review

    Clinicians stay in charge. In consultation sessions with 12 clinicians and 2 MDT coordinators, documentation support and case triage came out as the near-term uses; workflow integration and governance as the main barriers.

Closer to what the team chose

Two oncology experts labelled 66 treatment decisions across six real cases. The local model recalled 2.3 times as many of the treatments the MDT actually chose as a cloud chatbot baseline (p = 0.020).
