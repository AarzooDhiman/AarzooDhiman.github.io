Hello, I’m Aarzoo

I build NLP and LLM systems that turn messy human language into evidence, with health as my strongest proof. I did my PhD at IIT Roorkee and a postdoc at UCL, and I now teach AI at the University of Hull while working on an NHS-funded edge AI project.
