Room

The meeting that never leaves the room

A breast cancer team meets to decide treatment. My system listens, checks NICE guidelines and drafts options on one small computer in the room. The audio never leaves it.

  1. ListenWhisper large-v3, tuned for the room

    Silence-aware preprocessing, Bayesian search with Optuna and noise-adaptive decoding cut the word error rate by 20.7% and 24.4% on held-out recordings of real MDT meetings.

  2. RetrieveNICE guidelines, held locally

    Each case is matched against NICE guidelines in a local FAISS index, so a suggestion can point to the guidance behind it.

  3. ReasonMedGemma 27B, quantised

    Served with llama.cpp on CUDA and TensorRT inside Docker. Audio and transcripts stay on the device, in step with NHS information governance.

  4. ReviewClinicians stay in charge

    Consultation with 12 clinicians and 2 MDT coordinators found documentation support and case triage as the near-term uses, and workflow integration and governance as the main barriers.
